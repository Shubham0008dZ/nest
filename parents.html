Parents' Corner

Guidelines, transport, attendance, uniform and food policies — plus a little inspiration for the parenting journey.

Please read carefully

Guidelines for Parents

Compliance with the following is mandatory for a safe, disciplined and joyful school experience.

  • Attending school in proper, complete uniform is compulsory
  • Hair colouring, mehandi and non-regulation footwear are strictly prohibited
  • Girls with long hair must tie it neatly with plain black bands only
  • Mobile phones, excessive cash and jewellery/accessories are not permitted
  • Requests for short leave or half-day during school hours are not entertained
  • Unwell students must not be sent to school; leave after arrival is not granted on medical grounds
  • Long leave is sanctioned only for valid medical reasons with timely written application
  • Absence beyond 10 days without prior approval may strike the student's name off the rolls
  • Bullying, fighting, abusive language or misconduct invites strict disciplinary action
  • Students must carry all required belongings from home — items are not accepted at the gate
  • Parents meet teachers only by prior appointment on designated days
  • Birthday celebrations must stay simple and confined to classmates; gifts should not exceed ₹5 in value
  • All communication must bear the scholar number, name, grade and section
  • Check your child's almanac daily and countersign any teacher's note
  • Use the Edunext ERP app for syllabus, homework, circulars and fee details
  • Notify the school promptly of any change in address or phone number
  • Dress modestly and presentably while visiting the school; shorts/chappals are discouraged
  • Children should not carry sharp objects like knives or paper cutters
  • The school is not responsible for incidents occurring outside school premises or hours
  • Parents are expected to attend all PTMs and stay actively involved in academic progress
Getting to school safely

Transport-Related Behaviour

A dedicated fleet of GPS-tracked, CCTV-monitored, air-conditioned buses and vans keeps every commute safe and comfortable.

Before Boarding

  • Arrive at your designated bus stop 5 minutes early; board only your assigned bus
  • Stay away from the main road until the bus arrives
  • Parents/guardians must stay at the stop until the bus departs
  • Buses will not wait for latecomers

While Inside the Bus

  • Take your assigned seat and sit facing forward at all times
  • Keep heads, hands and arms inside the bus
  • Talk quietly — the driver's attention must not be distracted
  • Eating, littering or damaging bus seats is not allowed; damage is chargeable to parents

Attendance

  • 75% attendance is compulsory before Final Examination
  • 10+ consecutive unexplained absences may strike a name off the rolls
  • Sickness needs an MBBS practitioner's certificate within 3 days
  • Medical leave must be submitted via the Edunext App or email
👕

School Uniform

  • All uniforms must be clean, well-maintained and properly fitted
  • Polished black shoes are part of the regular uniform
  • I-Card is mandatory at all times within school premises
  • Only blue or black caps permitted during winters
🍽️

Food & Beverage

  • Fresh, hygienic meals prepared under strict quality control
  • Tiffin boxes & water bottles are not accepted at the reception desk
  • Sharing of food and water bottles is discouraged
  • Students must vacate the canteen promptly after recess
A little inspiration

The ABCs of Parenting

Small, everyday choices that help a child feel safe, seen and capable.

A – E

Accept & value yourself and your child. Be consistent, honest, fair and firm. Concentrate on what you like about your child. Develop interests of your own. Encourage your child to discuss ideas and goals.

F – J

Forgive mistakes — they're part of learning. Gladly share time, affection and support. Help your child feel safe. Inculcate respect towards all. Joyfully take pleasure in life.

K – O

Keep harsh criticism to yourself. Let your child experience natural results. Model the qualities you want to see. Negotiate privileges & responsibilities. Offer choices that build decision-making.

P – Z

Problem-solve together. Quit blaming & shaming. Respect your child's own pace. Share household tasks. Take time to read together. Value honesty. Zestfully join family traditions.
