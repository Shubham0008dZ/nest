Our facilities

A campus designed for curious hands

Infrastructure

State-of-the-Art Infrastructure

Nestled in a lush green campus, our school is surrounded by tall trees and blooming plants. Classrooms are smart-board equipped and air-conditioned, hallways are well-lit and well-ventilated, and an on-campus RO water plant keeps every Nipper hydrated safely.

🏫
Library

Junior & Senior Library

A vibrant, resourceful library stocked with fiction, non-fiction, encyclopaedias, novels and thesauruses — plus Kindle pads for a digital treasure trove of reading material.

📚
Sand Pit

The Sand Play Facility

Building, pouring and digging strengthen fine motor skills and hand-eye coordination, while shared play builds cooperation and turn-taking — introducing foundational STEM concepts like volume and cause-and-effect along the way.

🏖️
Roboshala

Roboshala — The Robotics Lab

A hub of innovation designed to ignite a passion for technology. Hands-on work with robots develops critical motor skills, mental reasoning, problem-solving, teamwork and analytical thinking.

🤖
Technology

Digital Smart Boards & Smart Surveillance

Interactive smart boards bring lessons alive with 3D models and real-time simulations, tailored to individual needs. Security cameras across classrooms and common areas keep every corner of campus safe and monitored.

🖥️
Excellence Arenas

Four Sporting Arenas & a 500-seat Auditorium

Premier facilities for Pickleball, Basketball, Box Cricket, Futsal and Volleyball, a stately auditorium for cultural pursuits, plus a PA stage and skating ring — a vibrant confluence of sport, artistry and self-expression.
