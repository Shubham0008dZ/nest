Admissions & Fees

Everything a parent needs to enrol — documents, steps, withdrawal policy and the full fee schedule for 2026–27, in one place.

Getting started

How admission works

1

Register online

Fill the online application form on our school website, or collect the prospectus and admission form from the reception (9:00 am – 2:00 pm, all working days).

2

Submit forms & documents

Complete the admission form and transport form (if applicable) and submit them with all mandatory documents listed below.

3

Verification

Our team verifies the original Transfer Certificate (for Class II and above) and all supporting documents.

4

Confirmation

Admission is granted once documents are cleared — welcome to Nestling Nippers!

📄

Documents required

  • Birth certificate (up to Class I), issued by Municipal Corporation / Local Bodies
  • Photocopy of previous class's Annual School Report Card, if applicable
  • Original TC for Class II & above (counter-signed by the board, if not CBSE)
  • Self-attested Aadhaar copy of both parent & student
  • Current address proof (rent agreement / electricity bill / IGL connection)
  • Permanent Education Number (PEN) & UDISE No. from previous school

Note: An affidavit is not acceptable as proof of date of birth. Admission forms & prospectus available at reception, 9:00 am – 2:00 pm. Queries: 0120-4111061 / 62.

🔁

Withdrawal (Transfer Certificate)

  • Submit a written application at the reception to withdraw a ward
  • Collect, fill and submit the TC Form from the reception
  • Absence is not treated as withdrawal — one calendar month's written notice is mandatory
  • TC is issued within 15 working days after result declaration, or 10 working days during session
  • Issuance is subject to clearance from Accounts, Library & Academic departments
