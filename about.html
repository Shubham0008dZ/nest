Our story

A passion that became a promise

From a single, prodigious idea to a luminous presence in NCR's education landscape — this is the journey and the people behind Nestling Nippers.

“ Founder Chairperson

Mrs. Shashi Jain

Aristotle said every action springs from one of seven causes — chance, nature, compulsion, habit, reason, desire and passion. The final shape of Nestling Nippers leaves us in no doubt: what inspired us was pure passion, evolving from a dream into a creditable reality.

We believe every child is a rare, unique, original masterpiece — and masterpieces are meant to be singular, never moulded into a showpiece. Young minds absorb what they observe, learn and follow, so we take a multi-dimensional approach to fortifying their lucid, impressionable years.

I thank you for your deep-rooted affinity and allegiance towards us, and look forward to an intensive and edifying year ahead.

“ Director

Ms. Atika Jain

For all that has been, thanks — for the future challenges, yes, let's blaze more trails in the sky. We welcome every parent, guardian and little Nipper with felicity to the 2026–27 academic year.

Our objective is to make every Nipper sure-footed and enlightened through a multi-faceted approach that promotes inclusive development. Looking forward, we envision our Nippers flying high and reaching the stars, while we keep striving to guard their intrinsic attributes.

Nestling Nippers continues to navigate new vistas and higher pinnacles, engaging unprecedented talent through deeper, more meaningful pedagogy. The wide horizon beckons us forth — we will continue the march.

What every Nipper promises

Our Code of Conduct

Five simple pledges, woven into every day at school.

🇮🇳

Nationalism

  • Respect our national heritage and take care of it
  • Never entertain thought, word or deed harmful to the nation
  • Imbibe national values and virtues
  • Value and respect the freedom of my country
🌱

Conservation of Environment

  • Preserve forests, gardens and fields
  • Plant trees and never cut them
  • Use natural resources judiciously — follow the 5 R's
  • Never pollute the Earth, its water or air
💞

Compassion & Respect

  • Love and respect all fellow humans
  • Share good fortune with those who have less
  • Love and protect all of God's creatures
  • Voluntarily contribute to organisations that serve society
🏆

Pursuit of Excellence

  • Stay committed to academic duties
  • Never compromise on academic performance
  • Perform to the best of my abilities, always
  • Turn weaknesses into strengths
🕊️

Honesty & Integrity

  • Never indulge in malpractice during assessments
  • Take responsibility for all work given to me
  • Stand by the truth at all times
  • Report any breach of conduct to my superiors
