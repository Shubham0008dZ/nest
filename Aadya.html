Aadya

Session 2026–27 · Playgroup Edition
By The Playgroup Newsroom

The Youngest Nipper With The Biggest Smile

Aadya joined Nestling Nippers this April, and within a week, she'd made friends with every child in her circle-time group. She's small in size but never short on opinions — especially about which colour crayon should come first.

"She waves goodbye to the same three toys every single afternoon."

Her days are full of small rituals: a specific song before snack time, a favourite spot by the window, and a stuffed rabbit that occasionally attends class too.
