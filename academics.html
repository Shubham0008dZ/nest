How we teach

Curriculum built stage by stage

From sensory play in Pre-Nursery to the inquiry-based XSEED system in LKG–Grade II, every level is designed for exactly where a child is.

Playgroup · Pre-Nursery · Nursery

Exclusively crafted & curated for our tiny tots

A warm, stimulating environment where young learners begin their educational journey with joy and confidence — nurturing early literacy, numeracy, creativity and social skills through play.

🗣️

Language & Early Phonics

  • Listening, speaking & joyful interaction through stories and songs
  • Introduction to phonics and sound recognition
  • Vocabulary building using familiar objects
  • Encouraging early literacy and a love for reading
🧦

Independence & Life Skills

  • Simple daily habits — washing hands, tidying up, self-care
  • Developing independence, responsibility, confidence
  • Concentration & coordination through hands-on tasks
✋

Sensory Exploration

  • Exploring the world through the five senses
  • Observing, comparing & understanding surroundings
🌎

Learning About the World

  • Colours, shapes, textures and sounds
  • Awareness of environment, culture & surroundings
  • Simple practices like watering plants
🏃

Physical Development

  • Motor skills through play-based activity
  • Running, jumping, balancing, coordination
  • Building strength, balance and confidence

The XSEED Curriculum

An integrated system combining curriculum, teaching and assessment. Moving beyond "teaching as telling," XSEED is inquiry-based and hands-on — every classroom becomes a laboratory.

Focus AreaWhat it means for your child
Values, Teamwork & LeadershipIntegrated daily in creative ways, with values attached to each concept taught.
Focus on ProcessA four-step process ensures every child participates, moving from concrete to abstract understanding.
UnderstandingExperiences are hands-on and kinesthetic — every classroom becomes an active laboratory.
Personalised LearningPersonalised reports and development plans are shared with parents multiple times a year.
Control of ErrorInstant feedback and corrective action help your child achieve true mastery.
